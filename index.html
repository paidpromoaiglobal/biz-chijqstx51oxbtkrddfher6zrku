Our Story

Lalimli Wala

Lalimli Wala is a well-known men's clothing store nestled in the historic Chandpole Bazar of Jaipur, Rajasthan. Conveniently located at 122, under Khandelwal Dharamshala, we have been serving the people of Jaipur with a wide range of men's suits, fabric, and garments at prices that won't break the bank. Whether you're looking for ready-made suits or quality fabric for custom tailoring, we bring you the best of both worlds under one roof.

With a curated collection of men's suiting fabric and ready-to-wear options, Lalimli Wala caters to every style and budget. Our store is open all seven days of the week from 10:30 AM to 9:00 PM, making it easy for working professionals and families to shop at their convenience. Located in the vibrant and accessible Chandpole market, we are committed to offering value-driven choices for the modern Indian man.

  • Wide variety of men's suits and suiting fabric under one roof
  • Highly affordable and budget-friendly pricing for all customers
  • Open all 7 days a week from 10:30 AM to 9:00 PM for your convenience
